Project Overview:
My project is going to create a gym simulation where the program is going
to keep track of the status of the different levels of fighters at the gym.
This software will demonstrate how a gym's roster and fighter experience
levels change over a decade.

Implementation:
I intend to use a std::map to handle the different levels of fighters across
different gyms. This map will organize fighters into three std::lists that
represent beginner, amateur, and pro fighters. The map will have the gym name
as its key. The map's value will be an array of those three lists.

A map entry would be for a specific gym. The three lists would represent the
beginner, amateur, and pro fighters in that gym. The two other map entries 
would be for two other gyms.

Simulated events:
The simulation will center on five different events: fighters moving gyms,
fighters taking a break due to injuries or other reasons, new fighters
joining the gym, fighters leveling up in skill, and fighters retiring. After
a set amount of time, fighters may switch or join gyms, take a break to recover from
injuries or other outside circumstances, develop in skill, or retire.
